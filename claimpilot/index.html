ClaimPilot takes the customer's first call by chat or voice. It then reads the damage from photos, checks cover in the policy system and screens for fraud, all at the same time. It prices and audits the repair, and waits for a human adjuster. Only then does it pay, book a partner garage and send the letter.

01 · The business problem

Claims are where customers decide whether to stay

A motor claim is the moment an insurer proves its promise. Today that moment takes days: a call centre form, photos waiting in a queue, manual coverage checks, a desk estimate and back-and-forth with garages. Meanwhile, recycled photos and soft fraud get through.

Cycle time

Days for a simple claim

Every hand-off adds a queue. The customer waits for news, and every call to ask costs the insurer money.

Leakage

Inconsistent estimates

Missed refinish, wrong labour rates or uncovered items get paid when there is no time for a second look.

Fraud

Recycled photos and new policies

Image reuse, losses right after the policy starts and frequent claimants are hard to spot by hand at volume.

    04 · Governance and safety

    Built for regulated claims handling

    These controls are enforced in code and covered by automated tests, not by prompt wording.

    No payout without sign-off

    The workflow pauses before settlement. A guardrail blocks the payment tool unless an adjuster approved that exact amount.

    Personal data masked

    Phone numbers, e-mails, Aadhaar and PAN numbers are masked before any text reaches a model.

    Decisions cite the policy

    Coverage comes from the policy system through MCP. Denial letters quote the exact clause and explain appeal rights.

    Rules in code, not prompts

    Coverage rules, fraud scores and settlement maths are deterministic code. The AI reasons and explains, but does not invent numbers.

    Full event trail

    Every agent step, tool call, partner call and adjuster decision is stored and can be replayed, alongside per-agent cost and latency.

    Fair to customers

    A high fraud score means "investigate", not "deny". Those claims go to the investigations team with the evidence, and no automated rejection is made.

    06 · Architecture

    Agents, systems and partners, each behind a standard interface

    Policy data comes through MCP and partner garages through A2A. That means real core systems and partners can be connected without changing the agent workflow.

    Experience

    Claims desk

    • React 19 + TypeScript, live over server-sent events
    • Chat with voice input and read-aloud
    • Live workflow graph, damage boxes, fraud evidence
    • Adjuster approval panel and letters
    Orchestration

    Google ADK 2 Workflow

    • Parallel fan-out, join, routing and a critic loop
    • Pause-and-resume for the adjuster
    • Sessions stored in a database
    • Gemini via AI Studio or Vertex AI
    Systems

    Tools and partners

    • Policy administration as an MCP server
    • Garage network as an A2A partner agent
    • Guardrail and telemetry plugins
    • Deterministic rules for money and coverage

    07 · Rollout plan

    Prove it on simple motor claims in 90 days

    Start where claims are frequent and low-risk, compare against today's adjusters, then widen the scope.

    Days 0–30

    Shadow mode

    • Run on closed motor claims and live claims in parallel
    • Compare damage, coverage, estimate and fraud calls with adjusters
    • No customer-facing actions
    Exit criteriaAgreement with adjuster decisions on most claims; no unsafe recommendations
    Days 31–60

    Assisted settlement

    • Live intake and assessment for one region
    • Every payout approved by an adjuster
    • One partner garage network over A2A
    Exit criteriaMeasured drop in cycle time and handling hours
    Days 61–90

    Scale

    • More regions and perils
    • Connect core policy and payment systems
    • Monthly review of accuracy, leakage and fairness
    TrackCycle time, handling hours per claim, leakage, fraud referrals, NPS
    Risks we plan for

    What could go wrong, and the control for each

    RiskControl
    Wrong damage reading from photosDamage regions are shown on the photo for the adjuster; estimates are audited by a critic agent before any decision
    Payment errorsPayments only after adjuster approval of the exact amount, enforced by a guardrail in code
    Unfair treatment of customersHigh fraud scores go to human investigators with evidence; no automated denial for suspected fraud
    Personal data exposurePII masked before model calls; private Vertex AI endpoints in your GCP project
    Partner or system outagesPartner and policy calls go through standard MCP and A2A interfaces with failures surfaced to the adjuster
