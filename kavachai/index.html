Forward a suspicious message. Get a verdict and a plan in seconds, in your language.

KavachAI reads what people forward: text, screenshots, links and voice notes. It explains whether it is a scam and what to do next, in Marathi, Hindi or English. Seven agents investigate in parallel, a deterministic risk engine scores the evidence, and guardrails make sure it only ever recommends verified helplines. With the user's consent, each report feeds Scam Radar, a live early-warning map of new scam campaigns.

01 · The problem

Scammers don't hack phones. They hack panic.

Fake police on video calls, fake traffic challans with malicious apps, "enter your PIN to receive money", "Papa, this is my new number". The scripts change every week and reach people in their own language. The person receiving them is alone, frightened and has seconds to decide.

Speed

Decisions made in panic

Scripts are designed to stop people from checking with anyone. A trusted second opinion has to arrive within seconds, on the app people already use.

Language

Warnings in the wrong language

Advisories are mostly in English, while many scams arrive in Marathi, Hindi or Hinglish, often as voice notes or screenshots.

Mutation

New campaigns spread before anyone notices

A new script, such as fake e-challan apps, can spread across cities long before it is widely known. Single reports are rarely connected.

    04 · Safety and privacy

    Designed so the assistant itself can never become a risk

    These controls are enforced in code and covered by automated tests, not by prompt wording.

    Verified contacts only

    Every reply is checked before it is sent. Any phone number or link that is not 1930, 112, cybercrime.gov.in, sancharsaathi.gov.in or an official brand domain is blocked and the reply is rewritten.

    Victim secrets masked

    OTPs, card, Aadhaar and PAN numbers are masked before any text reaches a model. The scammer's numbers and links are kept, because they are the evidence.

    Consent before sharing

    The workflow pauses after the reply. Nothing reaches Scam Radar unless the user says yes, and personal details are redacted server-side.

    Explainable verdicts

    Every point of the risk score traces to a quoted phrase, a domain check or a known-script match. The evidence ledger shows exactly how the verdict was reached.

    Never repeats the scam

    Replies warn about a malicious link without ever re-sending it, so the assistant cannot spread the scam it is warning about.

    Low false alarms

    Genuine bank OTPs, bills and delivery codes are hard negatives in the test set. Protective wording such as "do not share this OTP" is treated as advice, not a request.

    25 automated tests

    Every demo message end to end, consent share and skip, WhatsApp webhook and signature, contact guardrail, PII masking, radar clustering and link forensics.

    Evaluation

    56-message golden set

    37 scams and 19 genuine messages in four languages: 100% recall, 0% false positives and 97% scam-type accuracy offline, with 8 fresh messages written after tuning. Small and self-authored, so a field dataset is the next step.

    Interoperability

    MCP + WhatsApp

    Threat intel runs as an MCP server any MCP client can use. The WhatsApp Cloud API adapter verifies webhooks, checks signatures and downloads media.

    Risks we plan for

    What could go wrong, and the control for each

    RiskControl
    A scam is marked safeThe safe reply always carries a safety tip and never says "definitely safe". Thresholds are tuned for recall, and misses are added to the golden set.
    The assistant spreads a bad number or linkA contact guardrail allows only verified helplines and official domains; malicious links are never repeated.
    Personal data exposureVictim secrets are masked before model calls. Radar reports are consent-based and redacted. Private Vertex AI endpoints run in your GCP project.
    Over-reliance or legal misunderstandingClear "information, not legal advice" wording, and every urgent case points to 1930 and the official portal.
    Abuse of Scam RadarReports only come from completed investigations with consent. Campaigns need several similar reports before an alert, and alerts are reviewed by people.
